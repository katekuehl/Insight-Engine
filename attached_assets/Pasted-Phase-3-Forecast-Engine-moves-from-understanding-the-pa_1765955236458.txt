Phase 3: Forecast Engine - moves from understanding the past/present to predicting the future.
Phase 3 takes Phase 1 & Phase 2 insights and adds sophisticated forecasting to generate point predictions and uncertainty intervals for key business metrics.
Three Core Components:
	1.	Forecast Engine - The computational core implementing ARIMA, exponential smoothing, Prophet, LSTM neural networks, and ensemble averaging. Generates point forecasts and uncertainty intervals.
	2.	Forecasting Module - Orchestrates forecasts from multiple algorithms, applies uncertainty quantification, and selects the best model based on cross-validated error metrics.
	3.	Forecast Outputs - Packages forecasts with confidence intervals, trend decompositions, and anomaly alerts for business consumption.
Technical Scope:
Algorithms:
	•	ARIMA - Captures temporal patterns and autoregressive dependencies. Best for short-term forecasts (1-6 months).
	•	Prophet - Facebook’s algorithm for trend, seasonality (daily/weekly/yearly), and holiday effects. Robust to missing data and outliers.
	•	LSTM - Recurrent neural networks that capture complex nonlinear temporal dependencies. Best for high-dimensional complex interactions.
	•	Exponential Smoothing - Holt-Winters approach. Simple, interpretable, adapts quickly to recent changes.
	•	Ensemble Averaging - Weighted average of all 4 models. Weights determined by cross-validated error metrics (MAPE, RMSE).
Why Ensemble Forecasting?
	•	Single algorithm can fail catastrophically (ARIMA breaks on non-stationary data, Prophet struggles with outliers, LSTM overfits on small datasets)
	•	Ensemble combines strengths and automatically selects optimal weights
	•	Business planning depends on future scenarios, and uncertainty quantification is as important as point estimates
	•	Reduces risk that forecast failures cascade into bad business decisions

DAG Flow: Phase 2 Completion → Forecast Engine (ensemble of 4 algorithms) → Forecasting Module (aggregation, uncertainty quantification) → Forecast Outputs (confidence intervals, anomaly alerts) → Complete

Technical Requirements:
	•	Libraries: statsmodels (ARIMA, exponential smoothing), prophet, TensorFlow or PyTorch (LSTM), scikit-learn (cross-validation)
	•	Minimum 2 years historical data for seasonality detection
	•	Weekly or daily granularity (not monthly - too coarse)
	•	Computational targets: LSTM training ~3-5 min on 2yr data, Prophet ~10-20 sec, ARIMA ~5-10 sec, ensemble completion ~45 sec max
